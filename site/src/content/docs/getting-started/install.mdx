Health Relay is not on the App Store yet. You build it from source with
Xcode and install it on your own iPhone, which takes about ten minutes.

## What you need

- An iPhone with **iOS 17** or later. Workout effort scores need iOS 18.
- A Mac with **Xcode 27** or later.
- A **paid Apple Developer Program** membership. Xcode refuses HealthKit
  on a free personal team, and the background delivery that keeps your
  destinations current needs the paid program's entitlement. A development
  build signed this way runs for a year.
- [XcodeGen](https://github.com/yonaskolb/XcodeGen), which generates the
  Xcode project: `brew install xcodegen`.

## Build and run

1. Clone the repository and generate the project:

   ```sh
   git clone https://github.com/patsissons/health-relay.git
   cd health-relay/ios
   cp Local.xcconfig.example Local.xcconfig
   xcodegen
   ```

2. Put your team ID in `Local.xcconfig`. It is the 10-character ID under
   **Membership details** at
   [developer.apple.com/account](https://developer.apple.com/account).

   ```
   DEVELOPMENT_TEAM = ABCDE12345
   ```

3. Open `HealthRelay.xcodeproj`. In **Xcode › Settings › Accounts**, sign
   in with the Apple ID of your developer account.

4. Connect your iPhone, choose it as the run destination and press **Run**.
   The first time, enable **Developer Mode** on the phone when iOS asks
   (Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode).

5. In Health Relay, tap **Allow Health access** on the Status tab. In the
   sheet, tap **Select All**, scroll to the end and tap **Continue** (on
   iOS 26 and earlier: **Turn On All**, then **Allow**).

6. iOS 27 then asks how much history to share. Either choice works:
   Health Relay never sends more than the last 35 days, and it never sends
   a day from before the history you shared, so nothing at a destination
   is erased by a day it couldn't read.

   <Image src={history} alt="The iOS Health sheet asking how much historical data to share: Past 30 Days and Future Data, or All Recorded Data and Future Data" class="phone" />

Then [add your first destination](/getting-started/first-destination/).

## Keep it running in the background

Health Relay syncs on its own when Health records something new, and once
a day around 06:30. For that to work:

- Leave **Background App Refresh** on for Health Relay (Settings ›
  General › Background App Refresh).
- Don't force-quit the app from the app switcher. iOS does not wake apps
  the user has swiped away.

See [When it syncs](/guides/when-it-syncs/) for the details.
